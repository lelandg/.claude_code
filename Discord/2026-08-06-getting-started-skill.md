**New in my Claude Code marketplace: `/getting-started` — a concierge for people brand new to Claude 🎉**

My nephew asked me how to use AI agents for his business. Halfway through typing him setup instructions, I realized I was writing a skill.

So here it is. Install it, say "help me get started", and Claude interviews you — one question at a time, no jargon without a plain-English explanation:

- What do you want help with? (business, coding, writing, research...)
- What plan are you on? On a free trial, it switches to efficiency mode and stops burning your tokens.
- Already pay for ChatGPT, Copilot, or Gemini? It points you at the companion tools you already own instead of pretending they don't exist.
- Want to code but don't have an IDE? It suggests PyCharm Community Edition (free, and the AI plugins let you swap providers without swapping editors).

Then, with your permission, it looks at your setup, suggests improvements, recommends tools from my open-source catalog that match your actual goals, and hands you a personalized HTML action plan. Every command and starter prompt gets its own copy button.

Try it:
```
/plugin marketplace add lelandg/.claude_code
/plugin install claude-config-skills@lelandg-claude-config
```
Then just tell Claude you're new. That's the whole point. 😊
